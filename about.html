Origin

Why Ayushkama Exists

Our name is derived from Sanskrit: Ayush (life force / longevity) and Kama (desire/purpose) — embodying our mission to make life-enhancing foods accessible to the world while preserving the ecological systems and farming communities that produce them.

Our Sourcing Philosophy: Direct, Traceable, Ethical
We partner directly with 50+ certified organic smallholder farmers across four key growing regions: the Himalayan foothills of Uttarakhand for honey and herbs; the Jaintia Hills of Meghalaya for Lakadong Turmeric; the fertile plains of Tamil Nadu for Moringa; and the coastal landscapes of Kerala for Ginger and Banana. No middlemen. No adulteration. Full farm-to-container traceability with batch-level documentation.

"We saw smallholder farmers growing extraordinary Lakadong turmeric — 7% curcumin, among the highest recorded anywhere in the world — but with no pathway to international buyers. We built Ayushkama to change that."

— Founder, Ayushkama

Rooted in Uttarakhand's Agricultural Legacy
Our headquarters and primary processing facility are located in Uttarakhand — a state whose fertile soil, clean water sources, and traditional farming knowledge produce ingredients of singular purity. Uttarakhand's organic certification infrastructure and government support for tribal farming communities align with our values of sustainable, community-first sourcing.

Our Foundation

Purpose & Principles

Driven by Indian heritage, backed by global standards.

Our Mission

To empower India's organic farming communities by connecting them with global wellness markets — ensuring fair returns for farmers and uncompromised quality for the world.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted source for certified Ayurvedic superfoods — making India's wellness heritage accessible to every country that values clean nutrition.

Farm-to-Container Traceability

Every batch carries a scannable QR code linking to the farm source, harvest date, and COA. Our buyers never have to take our word for it.

Farmer Equity, Always

We pay above fair-trade premiums directly to farming communities — no middlemen. When export volumes grow, our farmers benefit first.

Zero-Compromise Quality

If a batch fails our minimum thresholds for curcumin, moisture, or heavy metals — it does not ship. Regardless of deadline.

Sustainable Export Packaging

Nitrogen-flushed, biodegradable packaging preserves bioactive potency over long transit distances and reduces environmental impact.

Global Regulatory Support

Comprehensive documentation including Phytosanitary certificates and Lab Reports for EU, US, UAE, and APAC import requirements.

Ayurvedic Integrity

Products are rooted in classical Ayurveda and validated by modern nutritional science. We only call it a superfood when both traditions agree.

Our Global Footprint
From a farmer-first start in Uttarakhand, Ayushkama now serves procurement teams and wellness brands in 23 countries — from the USA and Germany to the UAE, Japan, and Singapore. Our export team handles all documentation: phytosanitary certificates, organic certificates, export permits, and country-specific labeling compliance.
