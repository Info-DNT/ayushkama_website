Origin

Why Ayushkama Exists

The name draws from Sanskrit — Ayu (life, vitality) and Kama (purpose). It reflects our founding belief: that India's soil, shaped by centuries of Ayurvedic agricultural knowledge, holds many of the world's most powerful wellness answers.

"We saw smallholder farmers growing extraordinary Lakadong turmeric — 7% curcumin, among the highest recorded anywhere in the world — but with no pathway to international buyers. We built Ayushkama to change that."

— Founder, Ayushkama

Today, every product we export carries a QR-traceable journey from the specific farm and harvest date to the third-party lab certificate that validates its quality. We don't just source from India — we are built from India's soil up.

Purpose

Mission & Vision

Two commitments that guide every farm partnership, every product, and every shipment we make.

Our Mission

To empower India's organic farming communities by connecting them with global wellness markets — ensuring fair returns for farmers and uncompromised quality for the world. We measure success not only in shipments delivered, but in livelihoods improved.

Our Vision

To become the world's most trusted source for certified Ayurvedic superfoods — making India's ancient wellness heritage accessible to every country that values clean, conscious nutrition. A world where Ayurveda is not an alternative, but the standard.

Principles

What We Stand For

Six non-negotiable principles — in our farm partnerships, our processing, and our export commitments.

Farm-to-Container Traceability

Every batch carries a scannable QR code linking to the farm name, harvest date, and third-party lab report. Our buyers never have to take our word for it.

Farmer Equity, Always

We pay above fair-trade premiums directly to farming communities — no middlemen. When our export volumes grow, our farmers benefit first.

Zero-Compromise Quality

If a batch fails our minimum thresholds — curcumin content, moisture levels, or heavy metal limits — it does not ship. Regardless of order size or deadline.

Sustainable Packaging

Nitrogen-flushed, biodegradable export packaging preserves bioactive potency over long transit distances and doesn't add to landfill on arrival.

Full Regulatory Compliance

Phytosanitary certificates, Certificates of Analysis, and country-specific labeling support for EU, US, UAE, and APAC import requirements.

Ayurvedic Integrity

Every product is rooted in classical Ayurvedic texts and validated by modern nutritional science. We only call something a superfood when both traditions agree.
