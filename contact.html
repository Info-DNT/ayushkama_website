Common Questions

Frequently Asked by Importers

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQs vary by product. Most powders start at 100 kg per SKU. For seeds and whole foods, MOQs typically begin at 500 kg. We can accommodate smaller trial orders for new buyers — please indicate this in your inquiry.

Which certifications do your products carry?

Our products are certified USDA Organic, EU Organic (EC 834/2007), India Organic (NPOP), FSSAI Licensed, ISO 22000:2018, and APEDA Registered. Full certificate copies are available on request.

Do you offer white-label or private-label supply?

Yes. We supply in bulk to private-label brands and can accommodate custom packaging in various formats — from retail pouches to bulk sacks. Contact our brand partnerships team for a tailored quote.

How long does shipping take?

Sea freight transit times: US/Canada 18–25 days, EU 20–28 days, UAE 10–14 days, Australia 22–30 days. Air freight is available for urgent shipments. We handle all export documentation including phytosanitary certificates and customs clearance support.

Can I get a Certificate of Analysis (COA) before ordering?

Absolutely. We provide third-party lab COAs for all products covering curcumin content, moisture, heavy metals, microbial testing, and pesticide residue. Email quality@ayushkama.com with your product list.
