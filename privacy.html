Data Integrity & Privacy Policy

Ayushkama is committed to protecting the proprietary trade data of our international partners. This policy outlines how we handle sensitive business information, including Private Label formulations and supply chain data.

1. Trade Secret Protection

For OEM/Private Label clients, all custom formulations, brand artwork, and market-specific packaging dimensions are treated as strictly confidential. We do not disclose client identities to other competing brands in the same region without explicit consent.

2. Data Security

Inquiry data provided via our Technical Spec Request forms is encrypted and stored on secure servers. Access is limited to authorized export and legal personnel for the purpose of facilitating international trade.
