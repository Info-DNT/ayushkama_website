JOURNEY TO MARKET

4 Steps to Your Launch

01

Discovery & Selection

Consult with our export experts to select raw materials from our 500+ farmer collective. Define your grade and specific bioactive targets (e.g., Curcumin levels).

02

Sample Approval

We ship lab-tested samples to your facility for sensory and technical verification. Once approved, we move to pilot batch production.

03

Design & Compliance

Our team reviews your packaging artwork against destination market regulations (FDA/EFSA) to prevent customs delays and ensure transparency.

04

Export Logistics

The final product is Nitrogen-flushed or Vacuum-sealed and dispatched via FCL/LCL with all certificates (Phytosanitary, COA) attached.
