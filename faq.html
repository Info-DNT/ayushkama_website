Quality & Sourcing Assurance

Payments & Trade Compliance

For initial orders, we typically operate on 30% Advance (TT) and 70% against Scanned Bill of Lading. For established partners with high volumes, we accept Irrevocable Letters of Credit (L/C).

Yes, we provide Generalized System of Preferences (GSP) or REX numbers for EU importers to ensure zero or reduced import duty where applicable.

Currently, we provide DDP services for small-box shipments (air cargo) to USA and UK. For sea containers, we typically operate on FOB or CIF terms, though DDU is possible for specific regions.
